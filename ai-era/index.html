Manifesto & Field Notes

AI Era

Thinking clearly about the AI era: how to build AI that serves human goods, and what this technological gateway means for civilization.

I · Pillar 01 · The Next Gateway

AI’s potential to transform the world

AI as a one-way gateway, on the scale of the agricultural and mechanical revolutions. In deterministic natural systems such as proteins, materials, and energy, it can break the Red Queen’s Race of stagnating innovation. In self-reflexive social systems such as education and society, the same power behaves very differently, and that difference matters.

II · Pillar 02 · Tactical Product Ethics

AI ethics

Ethics as a design language, not a rulebook. No universal principle settles every conflict, so the real work happens in product design: building internal goods like agency, participation, mastery, and growth into the AI applications people actually use. Practical frameworks for teams shaping committed products, drawn from the classroom and the workshop.

III · Pillar 03 · Inner Resilience

Garden in the Citadel

Operating inside a technological gateway tests attention and judgment before it tests tooling. Classical Stoic practice — separating what is up to us from external noise, outcome anxiety, and role exhaustion — offers a durable inner citadel for builders navigating rapid transformation.

IV · Pillar 04 · Energy Architecture

The Architect’s Forge

The measure of AI-native work is not sheer volume of output, but whether automation reclaims human energy for pioneering thought. A well-architected workflow delegates repetitive maintenance while guarding the 20% where human taste, dialectic, and authorship live.
