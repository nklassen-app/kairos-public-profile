AI ethics

Ethics as a design language, not a rulebook. No universal principle settles every conflict, so the real work happens in product design: building internal goods like agency, participation, mastery, and growth into the AI applications people actually use. Practical frameworks for teams shaping committed products, drawn from the classroom and the workshop.

AI’s potential to transform the world

AI as a one-way gateway, on the scale of the agricultural and mechanical revolutions. In deterministic natural systems such as proteins, materials, and energy, it can break the Red Queen’s Race of stagnating innovation. In self-reflexive social systems such as education and society, the same power behaves very differently, and that difference matters.
